# Crypt - AI-Powered Trading Analytics Platform

## Project Proposal

### Executive Summary

Crypt is an AI-powered trading analytics platform that combines multi-model predictions, agentic AI assistants, and real-time market data to help traders make informed decisions. Unlike traditional trading platforms, Crypt employs a team of specialized AI agents that collaborate to provide comprehensive market analysis, risk assessment, and personalized suggestions.

### Problem Statement

1. **Information Overload**: Traders face overwhelming amounts of data from multiple sources
2. **Fragmented Tools**: Analysis, news, and portfolio tracking often require separate platforms
3. **One-Size-Fits-All**: Most platforms don't adapt to individual risk profiles
4. **Blind Recommendations**: Traditional systems give "buy/sell" signals without explaining why

### Solution

Crypt addresses these challenges through:

1. **Multi-Agent AI System**: 5 specialized AI agents that work together:
   - 📈 Market Analyst: Technical analysis and trend identification
   - 📰 News Scout: News aggregation and sentiment analysis
   - ⚠️ Risk Manager: Risk assessment and danger alerts
   - 🎯 Strategy Coach: Trading strategy recommendations
   - 💼 Portfolio Bot: Portfolio tracking and optimization

2. **Multi-Model Aggregation**: Combines predictions from multiple ML models for higher accuracy

3. **Risk-Aware Suggestions**: Actions tailored to Conservative, Balanced, or Aggressive profiles

4. **Explainable AI**: Every recommendation includes reasoning, model agreement, and agent opinions

### Key Features

#### 1. Market Overview
- Real-time indices (S&P 500, NIFTY, NASDAQ)

#### 2. Model Predictions

- Contextual responses

#### 5. Portfolio Management
- Holdings tracking
- P/L calculations
- Diversification scoring
- Rebalancing suggestions
